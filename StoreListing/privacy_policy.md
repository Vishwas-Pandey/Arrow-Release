# Privacy Policy — Release the Arrow

*Last updated: [fill in date before publishing]*

Virevia ("we," "us") developed Release the Arrow (the "Game"). This policy
explains what data the Game collects and how it's used.

## Summary

As currently published, Release the Arrow collects no personal data and
sends no data off your device. All game progress is stored locally on your
device only.

## Data we collect

**Locally stored, never transmitted:**
- Your level progress (highest level reached, in-progress puzzle state)
- Your settings (music/sound/vibration on or off)

This data is stored only in the app's local save file on your device. We
do not receive it, store it on any server, or share it with anyone.

**We do not currently collect:**
- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Analytics or usage data sent to any server
- Advertising data

## Advertising (if enabled in a future update)

A future version of this Game may show optional ads to let you continue a
level after losing all your lives, provided through Google AdMob. If and
when that happens, Google may collect device and advertising identifiers
to serve and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

We will update this policy and the app's Play Store data safety
declaration before shipping any version that includes real advertising,
and will note the version number here from which it applies.

## Children's privacy

This Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect any data from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. Continued use of the Game
after an update means you accept the revised policy.
