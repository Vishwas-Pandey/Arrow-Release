Privacy Policy — Release the Arrow

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Virevia ("we," "us") developed Release the Arrow (the "Game"). This policy explains what data the Game collects and how it's used.

Summary

As currently published, Release the Arrow collects no personal data and sends no data off your device beyond what is described below for advertising. Game progress is stored locally on your device.

Data we collect

Locally stored, never transmitted:

  • Your level progress (highest level reached, in-progress puzzle state, star ratings earned)
  • Your settings (music/sound/vibration on or off)

This data is stored only in the app's local save file on your device. We do not receive it, store it on any server, or share it with anyone.

Advertising

The Game shows optional ads (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads that let you continue a level after losing all your lives) provided through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required by applicable law (including the EU/UK and similar frameworks), we request your consent before showing personalized ads via a consent form shown on first launch.

Children's privacy

This Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
